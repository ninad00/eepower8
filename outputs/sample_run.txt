   1  add p q Zb       add a branch / new bus  (Type 1-4 found automatically)
   2  remove p q [Zb]  remove a branch (line outage)

   4  delbus k         remove bus k together with its branches
   5  show             display the present Z-bus
   6  branches         list the branches
   7  verify           check Z-bus against inv(Ybus)
   8  undo             undo the last change
   9  save             save Z-bus to zbus_result.mat / .txt
   0  exit
